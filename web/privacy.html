Privacy policy

Survey Cleaner · last updated 14 November 2026

Your survey data stays on your device

Survey Cleaner processes the files you choose (data exports, XLSForms and rules files) entirely inside the app on your phone or computer. Those files and the results are never uploaded to any server, and the developer cannot see them.

What is downloaded

The first time you open the app it downloads the program files it needs (the Python engine and its data libraries) from public software servers (cdn.jsdelivr.net and pypi.org). Those servers see a normal download request from your device, such as its IP address, as with any website. No survey data is sent with these requests.

What is collected

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your results

Cleaned files are saved only where you choose to download them. Closing the app clears them from its memory.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/emali-alfred/kobo-survey-cleaner.
