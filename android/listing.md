## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Survey Cleaner turns a raw KoboToolbox or ODK export into analysis-ready data,
plus a data quality report you can share with your team, in one tap.

Your data stays on your phone. Everything runs on your device: survey data is
never uploaded, and there are no accounts, ads or tracking. After the first
start it also works offline, in the field.

WHAT IT DOES
• Removes duplicate submissions
• Recodes missing codes (-99, -88, "don't know", "refused") to real missing values
• Tidies column names (hh_info/livestock/cattle_owned → cattle_owned)
• Converts numbers and dates using your XLSForm, and counts unreadable entries
• Splits multi-select answers into 0/1 columns and adds readable labels
• Flags extreme values for checking (never deletes them)
• Runs your own logic checks, e.g. cattle sold ≤ cattle owned
• Summarises each enumerator: interview length, rushed interviews, missing answers and flags
• Adds interview duration from start and end times

WHAT YOU GET
• Clean data as Excel and CSV
• A data quality report (HTML and Markdown)
• Lists of records to call back and values to confirm
• Everything in one zip file to share

HOW TO USE
1. Download your data export (CSV or Excel) from KoboToolbox or ODK
2. Open it in Survey Cleaner, optionally with your XLSForm and a rules file
3. Tap "Clean data" and download the results

Try it straight away with the built-in demo survey (synthetic data).

Built for field teams, M&E officers, researchers and data analysts working with
household, agricultural, livestock and socioeconomic surveys.

Free and open source:
